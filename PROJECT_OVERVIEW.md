## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Start Small:** Don't try to perfect everything. Launch with 3-5 projects, then add more.

2. **High Quality:** Your portfolio projects are your sales tool. Show only your best work.

3. **Regular Updates:** Add new work quarterly to keep the site fresh.

4. **Analytics:** Track where visitors come from and what they click.

5. **Contact Follow-up:** Respond quickly to inquiries to convert leads.

6. **Social Proof:** Testimonials are powerful - collect them actively.

7. **Call to Action:** Make it easy for people to hire you (clear buttons, working contact info).

8. **Mobile First:** Most visitors will be on mobile - ensure it looks great.
